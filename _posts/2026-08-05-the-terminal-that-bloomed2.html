🌿 The Architecture That Bloomed

The result was the DeepBloom Terminal—a component-based, data-driven platform where views are just lenses over a single source of truth, multiplexed through reusable components. Dot notation became our shared language. dividends.top, news.analyst, main.dashboard—each one a precise coordinate in a growing garden of intelligence.

We built a component taxonomy: data-table, card-news, calendar-box, list-compact. Each one a reusable pattern. Each one a petal in the bloom.
