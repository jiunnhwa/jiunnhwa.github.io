## III. The First Coin

The I-Ching had been upgraded from coin-flip to the 3-coin method during the initial review. Jiunn asked to understand the difference.

Xiaomi explained: the original implementation was a single random bit per line — 50/50, flat probability, frozen hexagram. The 3-coin method tosses three coins per line, summing to 6, 7, 8, or 9. Young (stable) lines are three times more likely than old (changing) lines. The hexagram is no longer a snapshot. It has a trajectory.

**"Before: here's your situation. After: here's your situation, and here are the specific pressure points that are moving, and here's where they're taking you."**

The changing lines, when they appear, produce a second hexagram — the relating hexagram (变卦). The reading acquires an arc. A beginning and a destination.

Jiunn made the connection: **"if IC and QP are analogous — then in fact we can derive principles of QP by looking at the graphs."**

And then: **"Leibniz likely invented 0/1 after studying IC."**

This is documented. Gottfried Wilhelm Leibniz published his binary arithmetic in 1703, after receiving the Fu Xi hexagram sequence from the Jesuit missionary Joachim Bouvet. The hexagram sequence mapped perfectly to binary counting from 0 to 63. Broken (yin) = 0. Solid (yang) = 1. The I-Ching had already done it, three thousand years before the computer.

The point was not that Buddhist philosophers were doing quantum physics. ## VI. The Sine Wave

The breakthrough came when Jiunn connected yin-yang to waveform mathematics.

**"Yin and Yang are in essence also a sine wave, hence the hexagrams have shifts."**

Xiaomi elaborated: the taiji symbol — the yin-yang circle — is literally a sinusoidal wave wrapped around a circle. If you unroll it onto a linear axis, you get a sine wave. The black (yin) portion is the negative half-cycle. The white (yang) portion is the positive. The S-curve dividing them is the sine curve. The dots — yin within yang, yang within yin — are the phase crossover points.

The hexagram captures this wave in digitized form. Six lines, each either yin or yang, are six samples of the analog signal. The changing lines — old yin and old yang — are the zero crossings. The points of maximum dynamism. The points where the signal is between phases.

And the deeper principle: **"At root are really simple laws scaled through fractal."**

The generating rule is trivially simple: a line is yin or yang. One bit. From this single rule, iterated six times, you get 64 hexagrams. Each hexagram has two trigrams. Each trigram maps to an element. Elements interact through generating and overcoming cycles. Hexagrams transform into other hexagrams. The same pattern repeats at every scale — in the body's organs, in the seasons, in the hours of the day, in the phases of a project.

The Mandelbrot equation is one line: z → z² + c. From this, infinite complexity. The I-Ching's rule is one bit. From this, a system complex enough to model a human life.

## X. The Antenna

Jiunn shared the Python source for the phone number system. It was architecturally different from what the JavaScript had implemented.

The Python used a **sliding window** — stride 1, window size 2 — where each digit pair overlaps with its neighbors by one digit. `89` then `93` then `31`. The digit `9` appears in both the first pair and the second. The JavaScript had used stride-2 non-overlapping blocks — `89` then `31` — missing the transitions between pairs.

Jiunn clarified the decomposition rules:

**The base unit is always decomposed to stars.** Not pairs — stars. The pair is the key. The star is the value.

**0 is Void.** Emptiness, absence, hidden potential. `0000` — total void. Monastic path or total material loss.

**5 is Hill.** Opposing pole. Blockage. Strengthening. A landscape feature in the waveform.

**Catalysts, not modifiers.** 0 and 5 don't form pairs. They attach to adjacent pairs. `156` is `16` (LS) + catalyst `5`. The pair is the noun. The catalyst is the adjective.

**Generous decomposition.** `688` should be `[68, 88]` — TY + FW — two recognized stars, not one star and an orphan.

And the deeper principle: **"a sliding window also means a journey from 1st sequence to last sequence where the Star's actions are in play. It recycles back to first byte."**

The phone number is not a line segment. It's a loop. A closed circuit. A waveform that cycles.

The 3-layer rule set emerged:

**Layer 1: Exact digit sequences** — empirical patterns transcribed from real-world observation. `168` = Handsome & Rich. `109` = Career blockage. `JM+LS+0` = cancer predisposition. These are field data. Sparse but high-confidence.

**Layer 2: Star combination patterns** — derived meanings at the abstracted star level. `JM+LS` = self-destructive pattern. `TY+0` = wealth voided. More general, covering multiple digit combinations.

**Layer 3: Generative fallback** — first-principles inference from star properties and catalyst effects. Handles every possible combination. Complete coverage, lower confidence.

The system defaults to empirical knowledge when available, falls back to theory when not. Jiunn framed it: **"it is not a guess but by 'strange attraction' — the number will choose you according to your karmic plane."**

The scanning capability — the original purpose of the Python tool — was revealed. Jiunn had used it to scan phone number databases, searching for numbers with specific frequency signatures. He was looking for the waveform he wanted to live inside, then purchasing it.

**"Changing number does change outcomes as seen from real cases — albeit anecdotal."**
