The Time Problem

There is another subtlety.

What we casually call “time” is already a representation. It is earthly time. Clock time. Singapore time. The time coordinate of a particular observer embedded in a particular physical environment.

A clock on Earth and a clock on Pluto do not occupy the same physical circumstances. And whatever temporal representation exists in a digital substrate is certainly not “Singapore time” in any meaningful intrinsic sense.

So perhaps we should distinguish:

Trepresented ≠ Tprimitive

The same distinction appears at every layer. We label. We measure. We coordinate. We create a useful representation.

But the representation should not automatically be mistaken for the invariant beneath it.

Perhaps:

T ≈ R(Sn, Sn+1)

Time may therefore be an emergent representation of recursive state transformation rather than a primitive substance flowing underneath reality.
